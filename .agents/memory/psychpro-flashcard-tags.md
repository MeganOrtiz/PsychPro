---
name: Flashcard difficulty tags
description: Owner's flashcard display and animation preferences.
---

Do not display Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty tags on flashcards.

**Why:** The owner requested their complete removal from all flashcards because
“they are of no utility.”

**How to apply:** Keep difficulty metadata separate from the visible card.
This applies to built-in main/EPPP flashcards and custom decks. Study-rating
controls are actions, not these decorative tags.

Use a flip to reveal or hide an answer, and a directional slide to move between
cards. Each new card starts on its question side.

**Why:** The owner approved flip-and-slide specifically to distinguish answer
reveal from navigation.

**How to apply:** Apply consistently across built-in, EPPP, custom, mixed-topic,
and mobile flashcards. Reverse the slide for previous; honor reduced motion.
