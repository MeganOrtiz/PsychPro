name: Textbook feedback promotion
description: Owner-approved distinction between website feedback rewards and book reviews.
---

The textbook promotion rewards honest feedback about the PsychPro website, not a review of the book. The owner approved a complimentary digital edition after feedback submission, with one claim per account and printed copies purchased separately. Positive and negative feedback are equally eligible.

**Why:** The owner corrected the initial assumption about public book reviews and explicitly approved the website-feedback promotion approach.

**How to apply:** The sidebar Feedback entry and a future dashboard promotion button should use the same feedback form. Digital book access belongs in My Library. Do not implement a book-review requirement, a positive-rating requirement, or printed-copy fulfillment as part of this promotion. Publishing submitted comments as testimonials requires separate permission and disclosure of the incentive.
