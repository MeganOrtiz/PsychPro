name: PsychPro suite identities
description: Owner direction for distinguishing the two suites and interpreting the PsychPro artwork reference.
---

Present PsychPro Suite and EPPP Mastery Suite as distinct learning offerings, not as one EPPP-only product. Their identifiers are a small softly glowing turquoise brain and a small softly glowing turquoise crown, respectively; repeat these identifiers on relevant landing feature cards.

**Why:** The owner wants visitors arriving from an external studying platform to immediately understand which offering each feature belongs to.

**How to apply:** Keep suite names visible alongside identifiers and give both offerings comparable prominence. Do not infer feature membership without checking.

When the owner refers to the “PsychPro design” for these suite cards, they mean the flowing turquoise glass/cloud artwork behind the floating landing-page brain, not the floating chrome brain itself. Adapt its composition to each card rather than copying the full hero literally.

**Why:** The owner explicitly clarified this reference and asked for an intuitive, aesthetically appropriate adaptation.

**How to apply:** Explore the artwork as card framing or a composed artwork region; preserve readable content. Canvas approval precedes live landing integration.
