---
name: PsychPro bot-protection preference
description: Keep abuse prevention low-friction and avoid human-verification challenges.
---

**Rule:** The owner does not want a visible “are you human” challenge. Prefer quiet, server-side protections, and keep shared-IP limits generous enough for students using school or library networks.

**Why:** The owner wants to deter bots without making the site feel hostile or blocking legitimate users on shared networks.

**How to apply:** Use authentication-provider protections and account-aware API limits first. Do not add a user-facing challenge or restrictive IP cap without explicit approval; review any plan-gated signup controls for shared-network impact.
