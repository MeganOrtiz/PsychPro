name: PsychPro two-plan pricing and trials
description: Owner-approved public plan simplification, separate opt-in trials, AI allowances, legacy protection, and expiry preservation.
---

Public choices are one combined **PsychPro** subscription ($19.99/month or $159.99/year) and the separately purchased **EPPP Mastery Suite**. Free/Master/Scholar are no longer new-customer plan choices; legacy billing/access compatibility must remain.

**Why:** the owner approved fewer tiers and a free week instead of a permanent free study tier, and explicitly selected the existing Scholar price for the combined plan.

Each suite has its own once-per-account, explicit-start, seven-day trial. Signup, onboarding, navigation, or selecting a suite must never start a countdown. Trials require no card and never auto-charge. Starting one suite must not start or unlock the other.

The PsychPro free week includes five AI generation units; the new paid PsychPro plan includes fifty per month, including annual subscriptions. One selected output tool consumes one unit. Failed generation must not consume allowance; deleting a generated deck must not restore it.

Existing paid subscribers keep their prices, access, and previous generation policy. Do not silently convert legacy plans to the new limits. Keep the new billing catalog invisible to older published pricing code during rollout.

**Initial launch context:** on 2026-10-14, the owner clarified, “there are no current users so we dont have to worry about that.”
**Why:** protecting existing subscribers is therefore not a blocker for this initial rollout; new-customer trial and payment verification still matters. This is launch context, not a standing claim about future user counts or permission to remove legacy compatibility.

Expiry pauses studying, new uploads, and AI generation without deleting account-owned materials. Progress/history, personal notes, saved source downloads, and earned books remain available. Purchased/earned books belong to the account, not either subscription.

**How to apply:** preserve these independent access dimensions when changing onboarding, paywalls, billing, generation, or account retention. Keep PsychPro's aqua and EPPP's pale-yellow plan styling; leave unrelated dashboard artwork and spacing alone.
